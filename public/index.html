LiveFleet tracking

Lokkate

Fleet owners lose fuel and vehicles and cannot prove where either went. A tracker in the vehicle now records every trip, and the owner can stop it from starting.

  • GPS telemetry from physical trackers, turned into trips and driver history
  • Remote immobiliser for missed payments, acknowledged and audited
  • Cargo temperature, geofences and after hours alerts
  • Billing on Ghanaian payment rails, plus a native iOS app

In developmentStorage monitoring

Warden

Stock in warehouses and cold rooms spoils when temperature or humidity drifts and nobody notices until it is too late. Warden puts wireless sensors in every section of a store and raises an alarm the moment a reading crosses its limit.

  • Battery powered temperature, humidity and door sensors through an on site gateway
  • A live floor plan of every store, with a heat map across its sections
  • Alarms by SMS and email, with acknowledgement and a response log
  • A space planner that places sensors and prices the kit before installation

LiveFact checking

Kasagadi

A claim can reach hundreds of thousands of people on WhatsApp in a day. Kasagadi gives accredited checkers a place to investigate it and publishes each verdict with the evidence behind it. We built it for the Ghana Anti-Disinformation Coalition Project, run by Penplusbytes with funding from the Norwegian Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

  • A claim state machine from submission to publication, every step recorded
  • Invitation only onboarding for accredited fact checkers
  • Evidence stored as records on each verdict, not pasted links
  • Public marketplace filterable by verdict, topic and region

LiveDigital memorials

Live On Forever

When a Ghanaian family is spread across four countries, funeral contributions end up in a notebook and the photos stay on private phones. Live On Forever gives the family one page that records both.

  • Contributions collected in the platform, paid out to the family's own account
  • Memorial pages with custom sections, shaped to the person
  • Moderated tributes and condolences
