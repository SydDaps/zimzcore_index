What we do for clients

Three things, all of which we have shipped.

Operational systems

Work that moves through stages, with different people responsible at each one. Applications that get vetted, scored and decided. Claims that get assigned, researched and published. Roles, permissions and an audit trail of who did what.

Hardware and payments

Systems that touch the physical world and real money. GPS trackers streaming position data off vehicles. Commands sent back to those vehicles. Mobile money collection, subscription billing and payouts, reconciled against the gateway.

End to end delivery

From the first conversation to a running system with customers on it. We design it, build it, deploy it and keep it alive. We are small and senior, so you talk to the people writing the code.
